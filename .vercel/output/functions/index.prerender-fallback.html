Technical Training School

The Technical School offers a wide range of training courses covering Plant instruments, Communication protocols, Cyber Security, Distributed Control System, PLC, NCS etc. with complete hands-on practise.

We provide customized training for you based on your requirement.

The Training delivery is incorporated using latest methodologies. Making use of RDP protocol, Cloud and other enabling technologies.

Training can be conducted in classroom, Customer site, Affiliate office and online.

The Virtual Instructor Led Training program (online) launched in 2020 has been successful in delivering training across the globe with complete Hands-on Experience.

Virtual Instructor Led Training (VILT)

Virtual Instructor Led Training delivery will be in the virtual classroom with expert instructions, demonstrations and practice sessions.

  • Participants would attend live instructor lectures and demonstrations on MS-Teams
  • Participants will have hands on practice sessions by remote access to Training PC’s loaded with relevant software
  • Trainers would present their desktop on MS Teams and adopt step by step approach

RDP & Remote Support

The trainees will access the trainee PC’s using Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP) to practice what is being taught by the trainers.

In case of any problem faced by the trainees, trainers would remotely login to the trainee’s system and help them to rectify the problem.

What do Participant need to have?

Laptop/PC with additional monitor, if possible
A good internet connection to access Virtual System.
