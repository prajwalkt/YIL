# User Guides

## Administrator Guide
- **Dashboard**: Access analytics, manage bulk imports/exports, and view system health.
- **Error Center**: Monitor application errors and unhandled exceptions.
- **Branding**: Customize logos and colors in the "System Settings" -> "Branding" tab.

## Trainer Guide
- **Assessments**: Create, activate, and manage assessments.
- **Feedback**: View aggregated feedback scores.
- **Materials**: Upload course materials (versions are tracked automatically).

## Student Guide
- **Learning**: Access e-learning modules. Progress is saved automatically.
- **Certificates**: Download PDF certificates containing QR codes for public verification.

## Affiliate Guide
- **Management**: View allocated students and access relevant materials.

## Finance & TM Guides
- **Finance**: Approve/reject pending payments and monitor monthly revenue trends.
- **TM**: Schedule upcoming batches and allocate trainers effectively.
